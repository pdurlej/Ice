Fire 1.0.1 “Ignition”

Fire is a local, agent-first control plane for the space around your Mac's menu bar. This is the recommended Fire 1.0 release.

  • Context Scenes let Codex, Claude Code, or OpenCode propose approved behavior for the app, time, or battery context you are in.
  • Fireline shows one useful thing below the notch or menu bar, such as local AI quota status or a selected menu bar item.
  • Exact item selectors prevent the wrong status item from being moved when one app owns several.
  • Fire CLI and agent skill provide guided setup, diagnostics, and recovery.
  • Bounded XPC calls keep a stopped helper from hanging the local agent bridge indefinitely.

Fixed since 1.0.0

  • The approval sheet describes the complete Context Scene, including quota-only Fireline payloads.
  • Disabling or removing the active scene now hides its Fireline even when other scenes remain enabled.
  • Context controls and Fireline have clearer accessibility labels and quota diagnostics.
  • Agent setup guidance consistently points to Settings → Agents and the compatible Ice.app path.

Your existing preferences, permissions, saved layouts, update path, and approvals are preserved. For compatibility, macOS System Settings may still list Fire as Ice.
